[h1]Hellmatch CS2[/h1]
Retro 90s-style deathmatch running inside Counter-Strike 2. No plugins needed: the whole game runs in the map's own script.
To play: subscribe, then start it from Play > Workshop Maps (add bots with bot_quota), or join a community server running it.

[h2]What you get[/h2]
[list]
[*]32 classic deathmatch levels (FreeDM, from the Freedoom project), rebuilt for CS2
[*]Up to 64 players across 1 to 4 arenas: one arena under 16 players, two from 16, four from 32
[*]A new mode every round, the same in every arena: Free for All, Team Deathmatch (T = red, CT = blue), Team Elimination, Last Man Standing
[*]Old-school movement, weapons, items, doors, lifts and teleporters, with the classic status bar
[*]Every frag is a real CS2 kill, so the killfeed, scoreboard and rank plugins count it
[*]Random weapon starts: now and then an arena starts everyone with a pump-action shotgun, double-barrelled shotgun, minigun, missile launcher, polaric energy weapon, ripsaw, or the SKAG 1337
[*]Players joining mid-round take a bot's place instead of waiting
[*]Retro FM soundtrack: longer tracks in Free for All and Team Deathmatch, short ones in one-life rounds (turn it down with your music volume)
[/list]

[h2]Controls[/h2]
[list]
[*]W A S D: move (always running), Shift: walk, Mouse: aim, Mouse 1: fire
[*]E or Space: use (doors, lifts, switches)
[*]R or Mouse 2: next weapon, F: previous weapon
[*]While watching: Mouse 1 / Mouse 2 change player, E or Space change arena
[/list]
Chat commands (! or /): [b]!help[/b] controls, [b]!hud[/b] status bar size, [b]!hud scores[/b] hide the HUD while the scoreboard is open, [b]!spec[/b] watch other arenas, [b]!afk[/b] sit out, [b]!back[/b] rejoin.

[h2]For server owners[/h2]
[list]
[*]Map name: mg_hellmatch (load it by Workshop ID: host_workshop_map 3820099820)
[*]The map sets its own round rules while arenas run (mp_ignore_round_win_conditions, respawn on/off per mode, mp_teammates_are_enemies in free-for-all rounds), turns buying and money off, and disables the radar. All of this can be switched off with APPLY_CVARS in the script.
[*]Plugins that can get in the way: AFK managers, no-respawn plugins, spawn protection and teamkill punishment.
[*]CS2 bots play using the game's own bot AI; bot_quota fills empty slots.
[/list]
